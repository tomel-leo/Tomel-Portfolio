About
Me

LinkedIn ↗ Facebook ↗

I am Olugboye Ayomide Feranmi, also known as Tomel, a Registered Nurse and Registered Public Health Nurse with a strong commitment to clinical excellence, health research and data-driven decision-making.

I earned my B.N.Sc. (Second Class Upper) from Olabisi Onabanjo University and am currently completing my internship in the Department of Obstetrics and Gynaecology at Obafemi Awolowo University Teaching Hospital Complex (OAUTHC), Ile-Ife.

Beyond clinical practice, I support research and statistical analysis across maternal, neonatal and public health studies, mentor nursing students, and contribute to community health initiatives.

My mission is to connect nursing practice with health informatics and data science, so that care is safer, more efficient and grounded in evidence.
